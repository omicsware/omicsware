Month 1

Shared Foundations

Study design, sequencing strategies, metadata, quality assessment, and reproducible project planning for genomic and microbial datasets.

Month 2 · Choose a path

Genome Assembly or Amplicon Profiling

Genomics: assemble and evaluate a genome. Metagenomics: profile microbial communities and interpret diversity patterns from marker-gene data.

Month 3 · Choose a path

Genome Annotation or Shotgun Profiling

Genomics: identify genes, repeats, and genome features. Metagenomics: characterize taxa and functional potential from whole-community sequencing data.

Month 4 · Choose a path

Comparative Genomics or Community Reconstruction

Genomics: compare genomes and study variation across samples. Metagenomics: recover population genomes and assess their quality and completeness.

Month 5

Specialization Project

Develop an end-to-end analysis plan in your chosen path, including biological questions, interpretation, figures, and a transparent workflow.

Month 6 · Capstone

Independent Genomics or Metagenomics Project

Complete a research-style project in one specialization and submit publication-ready results with a reproducible analysis report.

Months 1–2

Machine Learning Foundations

Prepare biological datasets, choose appropriate modelling approaches, evaluate performance, and communicate results responsibly.

Months 3–4

Modelling Biological Data

Work with sequence, expression, and clinical datasets; extract meaningful features; and develop interpretable predictive models.

Months 5–6

Deep Learning Fundamentals

Learn neural-network concepts for sequence and omics data, including model design, training strategy, validation, and limitations.

Months 7–8

Advanced Models for Biology

Explore representation learning, biological sequence modelling, multimodal data integration, and critically evaluate model claims.

Months 9–10

Reproducible Project Engineering

Organize a maintainable analysis project, document methods and decisions, test outputs, and prepare work for reuse by others.

Months 11–12 · Capstone

Build a Research-Ready ML Project

Design, evaluate, document, and present an original machine-learning project for a biological research question.

About the Founder

Dr. Nitesh Kumar Sharma

PhD in Bioinformatics from CSIR-IHBT, Palampur and former Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Southern California (Mangul Lab, School of Pharmacy), Dr. Sharma brings genuine research experience to every course module.

His published work spans metagenomics benchmarking (MetaWiz), RNA-binding protein prediction, genome assembly of endangered Himalayan plants, wastewater SARS-CoV-2 surveillance, and machine learning in clinical data. Publications in Nature communications, Genome Biology, Nature Reviews Methods Primers, iScience, Scientific Reports, PLoS ONE and more.

OmicsWare was born from a simple observation: most Indian biology graduates can describe bioinformatics workflows but cannot actually run them. Every tier is designed to close that gap.
